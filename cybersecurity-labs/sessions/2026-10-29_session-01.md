## 5. Key Lessons

- **Physical access beats passwords.** Anyone holding the SD card can reset the login.
- **Blocking ping hides a device from the internet, not from the LAN.** On a local network, Nmap uses ARP.
- **IP addresses change (DHCP). MAC addresses are the stable identifier.**
- **The first 3 bytes of a MAC are the OUI, which identifies the chip maker.** If the 2nd hex character is 2, 6, A or E, the MAC is private and random, so no maker can be found.
- **Tools have stale databases.** Nmap called 2025 OUIs "Unknown," so verify with a current source.
- **Trust protocol evidence (castv2) over fingerprint labels ("Ninja Sphere").**
- **Remote identification:** cause a known action (Live View), then watch for the matching network reaction.
- **Evenly spaced timestamps (every 30 s) mean automated behavior, not a human.**

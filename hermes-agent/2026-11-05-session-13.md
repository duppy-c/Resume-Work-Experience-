## Lessons

- An aggregator (OpenRouter) means many possible providers; set privacy rules at the account level, then check which provider actually served a request.

- Keep undo commands separate from the steps.
- `duppy_coder@hermes-pi:~ $` = bash; `❯` under the Hermes banner = Hermes.
